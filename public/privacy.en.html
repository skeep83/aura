What data we process

  • Telegram ID, name, username, and Telegram language — for bans, Premium, and to share your contact with a match when both of you tap "vibe" (mutual match only). We do not store profile photos.
  • Your stated gender, the languages you speak, and your search filter — to match you with other people.
  • Country — a country code from Cloudflare's network data (we do not store your IP address): for the "Passport" feature, the "Guess where they're from" game, and showing your match's flag.
  • Ratings, compliments, and salutes — anonymous: the recipient only sees the compliment or reaction itself, never who sent it.
  • Usage statistics — events like "chat started," "duration," "connection failed" (no chat content): to run the service and build the weekly "Aura Wrapped" recap.
  • Reports and warnings — participant IDs, the reason, and the time.
  • Payments — Telegram payment identifier, plan, and amount in Stars.

Video, audio, and messages

Video and audio travel directly between the two participants (peer-to-peer) or through an encrypted relay (TURN); we do not record them. Text messages pass through our server and are not stored; before delivery they're checked automatically — links and contact details are hidden, and messages containing threats, sexualization of minors, or extortion are not delivered. For moderation, roughly every few seconds the server receives a downscaled frame from your camera, checks it automatically (for nudity and signs that a participant may be a minor), and deletes it immediately.

Cards for Stories and Aura Moment. If you tap "Share" yourself, the image is uploaded to our server and available at an unguessable link for 48 hours — required for posting to Telegram Stories — then deleted. A shared Aura Moment photo is only created once both participants agree, and it is composed on your own device.

Artificial intelligence

Some features rely on an AI provider chosen by the operator (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or our own server): checking chat messages for serious violations, translating a message when you tap to translate, conversation prompts, and the text of the weekly recap. Only the message text or anonymized statistics are sent to the provider — never your name, Telegram ID, or video. The provider's handling of this data is governed by its own API terms of use.

Retention period

Account data is kept for as long as you use the service. Reports are kept for up to 7 days or until a ban decision is made. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties except where required by law. Payments are processed by Telegram.
